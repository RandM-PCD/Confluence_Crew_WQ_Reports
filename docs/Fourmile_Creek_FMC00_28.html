pH

pH is a measure of how acidic or alkaline the water is. Washington’s freshwater aquatic life criteria generally require pH to remain between 6.5 and 8.5, with limits on human-caused variation.

Turbidity (NTU)

Turbidity in nephelometric turbidity units. Washington’s freshwater turbidity criterion is based on the increase above background and depends on the designated aquatic life use.

Nitrate-N (mg/L)

Nitrate is a form of nitrogen that is easily transported by water. It occurs naturally, but excessive nitrate can contribute to increased aquatic plant and algae growth. Nitrate can come from soil organic matter, animal waste, plant decomposition, sewage, and fertilizer associated with runoff.
